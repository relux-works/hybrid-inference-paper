scenario, not an available purchase. NVIDIA's January2025 announcement gives
USD1999 as a historical launch price, used only as a cross-check. URLs,
short excerpts and the access date are retained in the JSON and R19 report.
The engine pins this evidence by SHA256 in run19-measurements.json.
